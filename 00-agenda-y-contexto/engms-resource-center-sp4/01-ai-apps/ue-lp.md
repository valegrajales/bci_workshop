# WELCOME TO Workshop - Building AI Apps & Agents - Learning Path

# Learn the Delivery

The **Workshop - Building AI Apps & Agents** engagement is designed to help identify knowledge gaps and build a digital capability and skilling plan that describes the knowledge customers need to achieve success with the adoption, operational, governance, and migration plans for this customer scenario.

Most workshop deliveries require one day of effort. This can be accomplished either as a continuous block of time or spread over a longer period, depending on customer availability.

To deliver this engagement:

- **Review the [Delivery Guide](ue-deliveryguide.md)** - The delivery guide provides guidance and resources to support you during the delivery.

- **Join the [Delivery Community](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/channel/19%3Adc692f3bec324088bbe5b9d07565c0f2%40thread.tacv2/VBD%20-%20Innovate%20Apps%20with%20AI?groupId=591c20e8-6a02-4476-8881-849bf672e6cf&tenantId=72f988bf-86f1-41af-91ab-2d7cd011db47)** on Teams - This community is used to get help, share best practices, provide feedback to improve this engagement and learn about upcoming changes.
