### Welcome to "Elevate your AI Apps & Agents with Hackathon​"

This hackathon is designed to showcase how AI Apps & Agents can be elevated through hands-on learning and innovation. By participating, your team will gain valuable experience that will accelerate your adoption of AI Agents solutions.

## Roles

- Specialist
- Technical Specialist
- Cloud Solution Architect
- Account Technology Strategist

## Outcomes

Participating in this hackathon will help your team to:

- Drive innovation with AI Apps & Agents.
- Gain hands-on experience solving real-world use cases related to your daily work.
- Reduce the time from idea to production for AI Agents.
- Explore Azure services that support agentic innovation.

## Tasks

- Build momentum and excitement around advancing AI Apps & Agents.
- Strengthen internal skills and knowledge in AI through practical activities.
- Demonstrate how AI Apps & Agents can address real business challenges and enable innovation.
