# WELCOME TO Solution Optimization for AI Apps & Agents - Learning Path

# Learn the Delivery

The **Solution Optimization for AI Apps & Agents engagement** is designed to enhance the customer environment by addressing issues related to Performance, Tuning, and Optimization (PTO). This comprehensive approach aligns with our AI optimization pillars: Governance, Performance Improvements & Resiliency, Security, and Monitoring

By participating in this engagement, individuals will gain valuable skills in optimizing AI workloads, ensuring efficient resource management, improving system performance, and maintaining robust security measures. This upskilling will enable them to effectively manage AI solutions, reduce operational costs, and enhance the overall user experience.

To deliver this engagement:

- **Review the [Delivery Guide](SolnOptimizationApps.md)** - The delivery guide provides guidance and resources to support you during the delivery.

- **Join the [Delivery Community](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/channel/19%3Adc692f3bec324088bbe5b9d07565c0f2%40thread.tacv2/VBD%20-%20Innovate%20Apps%20with%20AI?groupId=591c20e8-6a02-4476-8881-849bf672e6cf&tenantId=72f988bf-86f1-41af-91ab-2d7cd011db47)** on Teams - This community is used to get help, share best practices, provide feedback to improve this engagement and learn about upcoming changes.
