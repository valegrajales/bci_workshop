# Workshop - Building AI Apps & Agents

The goal of this engagement is to understand the skilling needs of the customer, to design and deliver a skilling plan that enables the customer team to successfully build and manage generative AI apps with ease. Some of the activities that might fall under this category would be:
- Identify skills and expertize required on Azure AI services and Azure PaaS to successfully build next customer POC or MVP.
- Establish a skilling plan to enable the customer team.
- Deliver an upskilling activity and/or guide the customer team.


## Objectives and Outcomes

**Objectives**
This workshop offers an opportunity for the audience to gain hands-on experience about how to combine Azure PaaS and Azure AI services to build AI apps through common AI technical patterns that aligns to customer industry and use case scenarios.Customers are likely to book this delivery to achieve one or more of the following objectives:
- help their technical teams learn more about generative AI offerings and the use cases in their industry.
- upskill their technical stakeholders in core concepts relating to the Azure AI offering.
- build knowledge on how technical teams can utilize generative AI models to build custom AI apps and agents.
- allow their teams to get hands on with generative AI technology


**Outcomes**
After attending the sessions, the audience should be able to work with CSAs or partners or on their own to:

* Execute Proof of Concepts (POCs) for building their custom AI apps/agents.
* Architect and design AI apps and agents using Azure AI Services and hosting them on Azure PaaS.

### In Scope / Out of Scope

**In Scope** 
Education sessions for building AI apps and agents modules should be delivered as scoped. For details on the delivery SKUs and modules available, please review the datasheet.For each module the delivery format is a mixture of...
- instructor-led education
- demos
- labs
- discussion
- Q&A


**Out of Scope** 
While we might touch some of the below topics to explain AI technical patterns and how to host your apps/agents on Azure, this VBD is not focused to
- Introduction to Azure AI services and generative AI in general.
- Azure AI Foundry product deep-dive hands-on labs on Azure AI Search, Azure AI Document Intelligence, Azure AI Document Understanding, Azure AI Vision, etc.
- GenAIOPS concepts and practices.

In such cases please work with your CSA to re-scope the engagement leveraging:
- WorkshopPLUS - Azure AI: Platform and Services
- Workshop - GenAIOps

Furthermore, If the customer would prefer to build a hands-on PoC tailored to their specific requirements, then the Proof of Concept activity in this scenario might be a better fit.

#### Technical Requirements and Pre-Requisites 
A list of demo resources is provided in this guide and you should test all demos before the delivery to ensure they work in your environment.

It is highly recommended to include lab activities in all modules. Some lab resources are available in this guide. Check the content provided in the [IPKit](https://aka.ms/YAOJ-IPKIT).

In order to run most labs successfully, the customer needs to - at a minimum - ensure that:

- All participants have access to an Azure Subscription
- All participants have Owner permissions on at least the Azure resource groups that the need to work with as part of the lab
- There is sufficient Azure credit/budget (50 USD or more recommended) per participant available to allow for experimentation and to spin up some more expensive data service
- All participants either have or can create a GitHub user to access and work with GitHub repos containing the labs 
- All participants have access to a compatible IDE (for example: Visual Studio Code) and a Git client (for example: Git for Windows) or a Microsoft Dev Box or Codespace with similar development tools
