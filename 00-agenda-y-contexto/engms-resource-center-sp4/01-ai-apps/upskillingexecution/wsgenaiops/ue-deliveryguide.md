# Workshop- GenAIOps delivery guide

The GenAIOps Workshop provides an in-depth overview of GenAI operations, covering AI workload operations, complexity levels, and best practices. It outlines the Enterprise GenAI lifecycle, which includes stages such as prototyping, design, operationalization, deployment, governance, and continuous improvement. The workshop also details the maturity levels of GenAIOps, from initial discovery to optimized processes, and highlights common challenges and solutions. Additionally, it emphasizes the benefits of GenAIOps, such as improved deployments, automation, and cross-functional integration, and provides practical steps for implementing and monitoring GenAI applications.

**Objectives**

- GenAI Ops Overview 
    - Common Best Practices  
    - Operating model Importance 
    - Benefits of GenAIOps
- GenAIOps Maturity Levels 
- Enterprise GenAI Lifecycle
    - Implementation of GenAIOps
    - Building use case Architecture
    - Monitoring and Cost Management

**Outcomes**

This workshop's main outcome is to equip participants with the knowledge and skills needed to implement and manage GenAIOps effectively in their organizations.
- Participants will gain a thorough understanding of GenAIOps, including its definition, operations best practices, benefits, and challenges.
- Participants will be equipped to implement GenAIOps effectively in their organizations.
- Participants will learn common best practices for operations and solutions to address GenAIOps challenges.
- Insight into the lifecycle of GenAI in an enterprise setting and understanding the different maturity levels of GenAIOps.
- Participants will learn about the benefits of GenAI Ops, including deployments, automation, cross-functional integration, reproducibility, reusability, and governance
- Participants will understand the importance of monitoring and cost management strategies
