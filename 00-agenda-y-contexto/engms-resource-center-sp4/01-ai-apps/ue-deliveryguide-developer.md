# Advanced AI concepts for App Developers
## Workshop Asset List

[Return to the main delivery guide](./ue-deliveryguide.md)

In addition to the core delivery content for this engagement, we have provided you with a list of demo and labs that you can use during your delivery. 

Please use the table below to find resources that work best for your customer scenario.

**Know of a good sample solution, reference, demo, or lab that is missing?** Please add it here via a PR.

**Alternative presentation content** is referenced for cutsomers who wish to explore topics not listed in the main modules of this engagement. 

### Find Supporting Content

The supporting content has been organized per modules. In some cases where delivery type is 'workshop', you can use the workshop material linked as an alternative to the [core modules contained in this workshop](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/teams/ASDIPRelease/AzureCloudAI/Forms/AllItems.aspx?id=%2Fteams%2FASDIPRelease%2FAzureCloudAI%2FSP2%20VBD%2FAI%20Apps%2FWorkshop%20%2D%20Building%20AI%20Apps%20%26%20Agents&newTargetListUrl=%2Fteams%2FASDIPRelease%2FAzureCloudAI&viewpath=%2Fteams%2FASDIPRelease%2FAzureCloudAI%2FForms%2FAllItems%2Easpx), if that better helps to meet the customer's upskilling requirement.
