# WELCOME TO Proof of Concept for AI Apps & Agents - Learning Path

# Learn the Delivery

The **Proof of Concept for AI Apps & Agents** engagement provides a functional, small-scale version of the solution, allowing customers to assess its feasibility and support design decisions. This enables you to demonstrate the feasibility of the architecture design, potentially including complete scenarios and integration with existing infrastructure, solutions, and applications.

On average the Proof of Concept takes 5 days of effort. This can be accomplished either as a continuous block of time or spread over a longer period, depending on customer availability.

To deliver this engagement:

- **Review the [Delivery Guide](poc-deliveryguide.md)** - The delivery guide provides guidance and resources to support you during the delivery.

- **Join the [Delivery Community](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/channel/19%3Adc692f3bec324088bbe5b9d07565c0f2%40thread.tacv2/VBD%20-%20Innovate%20Apps%20with%20AI?groupId=591c20e8-6a02-4476-8881-849bf672e6cf&tenantId=72f988bf-86f1-41af-91ab-2d7cd011db47)** on Teams - This community is used to get help, share best practices, provide feedback to improve this engagement and learn about upcoming changes.
