# Deliver technical proof and business proof

## Activity Description​

Deliver technical proof focuses on deep technical work to show how the technology meets customer needs, through engagements such as Proof of Concepts, Pilots, developing MVPs, prototypes, or tailored demos.

Deliver business proof, which is usually gained through relationship meetings, including customer meetings with Microsoft executives or with other customers who have signed similar deals.

## Roles

Specialist, Technical Specialist, Cloud Solution Architect, Account Technology Strategist

## Outcomes

The customer has received any proof work required as part of the solution and agreed that what has been proven to them meets their needs.

## Tasks

Confirm proof work to be done (initially identified in Inspire & Design).  Confirm who in the customer needs to approve the proof outcomes and who to work with to do the proof work.  Confirm the process they undergo to confirm proof (e.g., might be individuals that need proof, for them to present to others in the customer). Understand what type of proof will satisfy their need for what needs proving. Action doing the proof work with the customer stakeholder and help customer's team learn how to deliver similar solutions themselves.  Ensure proof results are communicated to all that need to approve proof outcomes in the customer.

## Content & Programs

Use TDM Pitch decks (Prod), Demos, Partner pitch decks, Product/Solution docs (incl IS/SA) to demonstrate to the customer the value of our solutions and Products. Also use Product Workshops, MTC, etc. to ensure detailed/customized solutions can be demonstrated as needed.
