
# Complete AI Production Readiness Assessment

## Activity Description
Before moving from PoC to production, a structured assessment is required to validate technical, operational, and organizational readiness for AI workloads.  
If gaps are identified, additional work must be planned and executed to ensure a smooth transition to production.

### AI Production Readiness Context
The customer is guided through an evaluation of their architecture, governance, and operational maturity to determine if they are ready for production deployment of AI solutions (including **GenAI** and **Agentic AI**) on Azure.  
This assessment helps identify risks, gaps, and opportunities for optimization and provides a roadmap for achieving production readiness.

---

## Roles
 
- Cloud Solution Architect  


---

## Outcome
The engagement results in either a **Production-Ready** decision or a remediation plan to address gaps preventing readiness.

### AI Production Readiness Context
The customer and account team use the assessment outcomes to define the next steps for scaling AI solutions in production.  
The readiness activity ensures the organization can **build, run, and support AI workloads securely and efficiently on Azure**.

---

## Task
Perform technical and operational checks across architecture, security, governance, and operational processes to confirm production readiness.  
If not ready, document gaps and provide an actionable roadmap for remediation.
