# WELCOME TO Architecture Design and Review Session for AI Apps and Agents - Learning Path

# Learn the Delivery

In the **Architecture Design and Review Session for AI Apps and Agents**, the technical members of the team develop the conceptual architecture for the solution and, where feasible, the implementation architecture or its key components. These implementation details will expedite the Realize Value stage, where the actual implementation occurs. These architectures will be included in the solution design presented to the customer. During an Architecture Review Session, you will collaborate with the customer to validate the architecture following a Proof of Concept or Pilot. After evaluating the production requirements, you will devise production plans.

On average the Architecture Design and Review session takes three to five days of effort. This can be accomplished either as a continuous block of time or spread over a longer period, depending on customer availability.

To deliver this engagement:

- **Review the [Delivery Guide](./ads-deliveryguide.md)** - The delivery guide provides guidance and resources to support you during the delivery.

- **Join the [Delivery Community](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/channel/19%3Adc692f3bec324088bbe5b9d07565c0f2%40thread.tacv2/VBD%20-%20Innovate%20Apps%20with%20AI?groupId=591c20e8-6a02-4476-8881-849bf672e6cf&tenantId=72f988bf-86f1-41af-91ab-2d7cd011db47)** on Teams - This community is used to get help, share best practices, provide feedback to improve this engagement and learn about upcoming changes.

- **Review the Delivery Standards** - At completion of the delivery, you must provide the customer with final production architecture document that can be used for production deployment.
