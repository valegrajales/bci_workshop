# Proof of Concept to Build and Deploy Azure AI Platform

Proof of Concept | 3 or 5 Days | Remote | Onsite | 300 - Advanced

## Description

This engagement is centered on demonstrating the feasibility and value of designing, building, and deploying machine learning solutions using Azure Machine Learning (AzureML), customized to meet your organization’s unique requirements. The initiative will illustrate how AzureML can be seamlessly integrated into your existing data and application infrastructure to accelerate innovation, automate workflows, and enhance data-driven decision-making. By leveraging AzureML’s robust capabilities—including automated ML, responsible AI tooling, MLOps pipelines, and scalable deployment options across real-time and batch endpoints—this engagement aims to provide a practical understanding of the platform’s strengths in real-world enterprise scenarios. 
The outcome will emphasize tangible business benefits such as improved operational efficiency, faster time-to-insight, and enhanced model governance. Ultimately, this engagement will lay the groundwork for broader AI adoption across your enterprise by aligning AzureML’s technical capabilities with your strategic goals.

## Objectives
- Demonstrate the feasibility of building and deploying machine learning solutions using Azure Machine Learning (AzureML).
- Tailor AzureML capabilities to align with your organization’s specific data, infrastructure, and business needs.
- Integrate AzureML into existing systems to streamline workflows and automate model lifecycle management.
- Showcase the use of AzureML features such as AutoML, MLOps, and scalable deployment endpoints.
- Highlight secure, compliant, and reproducible model development practices.

## Outcomes
- A clear understanding of AzureML’s real-world applications and enterprise-grade capabilities.
- Practical insights into accelerating innovation and enhancing decision-making with ML.
- Demonstrated improvements in operational efficiency and time-to-insight.
- A validated foundation for broader AI and ML adoption across the organization.
- Alignment of technical solutions with strategic business goals.

## Scope
This scope outlines the activities and deliverables involved in building and deploying an Azure AI Platform as a demonstration of feasibility and value.
- Assess current business requirements and objectives for the use case to be implemented with Azure Machine Learning 
- Design a high-level architecture for the Azure AI Platform tailored to identified needs
- Set up and configure relevant Azure services and resources necessary for the AI platform
- Develop and integrate selected AI models or services to showcase key functionalities
- Deploy the platform in a controlled environment for demonstration purposes
- Validate platform performance and provide initial testing to ensure expected outcomes
- Document the solution, including architecture, configuration, and operational guidelines
- Conduct a knowledge transfer session to review the platform and answer stakeholder questions
