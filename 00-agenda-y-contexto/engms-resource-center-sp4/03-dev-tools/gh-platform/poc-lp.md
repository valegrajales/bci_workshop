# WELCOME TO Proof of Concept for Migrating Code Repositories to GitHub - Learning Path

# Learn the Delivery

The **Proof of Concept** engagement delivers a functional, small-scale version of the solution that customers can then use to 
determine its feasibility and reinforce design decisions. This will enable you to demonstrate that the architecture design is feasible, 
It might include compete scenarios and integration with existing infrastructure, solutions, and applications.

On average the Proof of Concept takes 1 day of effort. This can be completed 
as a contiguous block of time or over a longer period of time depending on customer 
availability.

To deliver this engagement:

 - **Review the [Delivery Guide](./poc-deliveryguide.md)** - The 
delivery guide provides guidance and resources to support you during the delivery

 - **Join the [Delivery Community](https://aka.ms/DevSecOpsVBDCommunity)** on Teams - 
This community is used to get help, share best practices, provide feedback to improve this 
engagement and learn about upcoming changes.

 - **Review the Delivery Standards** - At completion of the delivery, you must provide the customer with final production architecture document that can be used for production deployment.
