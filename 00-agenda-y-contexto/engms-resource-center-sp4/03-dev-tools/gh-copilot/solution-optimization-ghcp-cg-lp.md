# WELCOME TO Solution Optimization for FinOps for GitHub Copilot - Learning Path

# Learn the Delivery

This engagement is designed to help identify knowledge gaps and build a digital capability and skilling plan that describes 
the knowledge customers need to achieve success with the adoption, operational, governance, and migration plans for this customer scenario.

On average this engagement takes **3 days of effort**. 
This can be completed as a contiguous block of time or over a longer period of time depending on customer availability.

To deliver this engagement:

 - Review the **[Delivery Guide](./solution-optimization-ghcp-cg-deliveryguide.md)** 
   - The delivery guide provides guidance and resources to support you during the delivery  

 - **Join the [Delivery Community](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/channel/19%3A2da84a09114b46449ca0278bf8354738%40thread.tacv2/VBD%20-%20Copilot%20for%20Developers?groupId=591c20e8-6a02-4476-8881-849bf672e6cf&tenantId=72f988bf-86f1-41af-91ab-2d7cd011db47)** on Teams
   - This community is used to get help, share best practices, provide feedback to improve this engagement and learn about upcoming changes.

 - **Review the Delivery Standards** 
   - At completion of the delivery, ensure the customer expectation and initial scope set is met.
