# GitHub Copilot Adoption Challenge

When developers are engaged through structured, fun, and competitive formats, they don’t just adopt Copilot, they become Advocates.
That’s exactly what we’re seeing with the GitHub Copilot Challenge, a 4-week gamified experience that’s generating real traction and excitement.

### Why a Challenge?
Developers are setup in different teams in a friendly competition to see who can complete the most challenges using GitHub Copilot.  Each challenge is designed to get developers to use GitHub Copilot in their real workflows or projects.  The challenges are fun, engaging, and educational.  They also help developers to see the value of GitHub Copilot in their day-to-day work. 

### Adoption Challenge Leaderboard App
To help manage the challenge, an app is created to track the progress of each team and individual.  The database is used to store the challenges, teams, and points.  The app also provides a leaderboard to show the current standings of each team and individual.  The app can be customized to fit the needs of the organization.  [`LeaderboardApp`](https://github.com/Azure-Samples/github-copilot-adoption-challenge-leaderboard) 

The Leaderboard standing can be used for the customer to acknowledge the participants and leading teams.

### Delivery Model
- Day 0: Planning and initial setup.  This should occur 1-2 weeks before the challenge starts.  This includes setting up the teams, challenges, and leaderboard app.

- Day 1: Kickoff and Excitement.  Walkthrough of challenges.

- Day 2 ~ 3: AMA - Demo, walkthrough of challenges, Q&A, and share best practices.

- Day 4: Last AMA.  Declare winners and celebrate success.  Share the results and feedback from the challenge.  Discuss next steps for continued adoption and usage of Copilot.
