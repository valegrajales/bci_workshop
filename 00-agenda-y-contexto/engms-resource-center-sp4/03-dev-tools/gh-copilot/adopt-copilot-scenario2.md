# 🤖 Awesome GitHub Copilot Customizations

[`awesome-copilot`](https://github.com/github/awesome-copilot) is a curated collection of prompts, instructions, and chat modes to supercharge your GitHub Copilot experience across different domains, languages, and use cases.  This adoption service can be used toward adopting `prompt driven development`  `agentic productivity` with GitHub Copilot.

### Scenario
Customer is looking to customize GitHub Copilot to better fit their development workflows and enhance productivity. This service will guide them through the process of tailoring GitHub Copilot to meet their specific needs by leveraging the power of agentic AI.  The custom instructions and prompts can be shared across the organization to help with adoption.  We strongly recommend guiding the customer and their internal GitHub Champions to create, contribute, and maintain their own `awesome-copilot` repo for their organization.  


### Delivery Model
- Day 0: Planning and initial setup.  This should occur 1-2 weeks before the adoption starts. 

- Day 1: Kickoff and Excitement.  

- Day 2 ~ 3: AMA - Demo, walkthrough of using custom instructions and prompts, Q&A, and share best practices.  AMA can be use to help with creating custom instructions and prompts for customer projects.

- Day 4: Last AMA.  Share the results and feedback from the customizations.  Discuss next steps for continued adoption and usage of Copilot.
