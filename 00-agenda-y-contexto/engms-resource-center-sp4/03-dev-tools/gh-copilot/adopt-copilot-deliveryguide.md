# Delivery Guide: GitHub Copilot Adoption and Value Realization
This guide provides details and instructions to help you deliver the **GitHub Copilot Adoption and Value Realization** service for your customer as a 4 day Value-Based Delivery (VBD) engagement.
This engagement is designed to maximize the value of GitHub Copilot Business or Enterprise through proven adoption strategies, hands-on enablement, and tailored guidance.
The delivery must accomplish the following objectives:


**Objectives**

- Establish comprehensive GitHub Copilot adoption strategy aligned with business objectives
- Enable development teams with hands-on GitHub Copilot skills and best practices
- Measure and demonstrate measurable productivity improvements and ROI
- Build internal champions and support networks across development teams


**Deliverable Standards**

- Successfully help adopt scoped GitHub Copilot features across the organization(s), with a focus on value realization
- Use GitHub's [Engineering System Success Playbook (ESSP)](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/get-started/achieve-company-goals) to drive meaninful and measurable improvements
- Get customer leadership commitment to GitHub Copilot adoption
- Provide actionable feedback on business readiness, deployment, and governance
- Deliver a close out deck summarizing outcomes, learnings, and value delivered 

>Note: These are baseline requirements that may be further specified during scoping. Always deliver against the agreed scope.


## Customer Participants
`Note: Provide details on the customer participants that are needed for a successful delivery `

Having the right customer participants in the Adoption Service engagement is essential to completing a successful VBD. Ensure the customer roles outlined below are available and able to participate.

|Customer Role | Description | Required or Optional|
|----|----|----|
|Customer Relationship Holder| The person(s) responsible for the deliver on the customer site | Required (at least kick off and close out)
|Developers and similar roles| Developers who will use GitHub Copilot. You may want to interview them about their experience to gather evidence for your close out. | As Required
|Internal GitHub Champions| These are the people who will help drive adoption and support other developers. They may be team leads, architects, or other influential developers. | Highly Recommended
|GitHub Organizational Admin| The owner of the GitHub organization where GitHub Copilot will be enabled | Required
|Other IT professionals| These are the people responsible for provisioning and managing IT and Azure resources. You may need access to them to configure deployment to developer IDEs.| As Required|


## Delivery Guidance
This section includes delivery guidance and supporting artifacts. You are free to adapt each step to meet your customer's needs as long as you are able to meet the objectives and deliverable standards. 
These are the minimum requirements for a successful GitHub Copilot adoption and value realization engagement.

You may not be able to verify all of the prerequisites listed during the assessment and may rely on the customer's responses in these checklists in some cases; 
Accurate information is however very important and you may want to lean on the account team if you need a second opinion on any aspects of the customer's responses. 


### Step 1: Scoping
Use the scoping deck, prerequisites checklist, and datasheet to explain how the delivery will be carried out and what the customer can expect at each phase. Request the customer roles needed for a successful engagement (see [Customer Participants](#customer-participants)). ***Read the Field Engagement Guide *** in folder for the [GitHub Copilot Adoption and Value Realization](https://aka.ms/GHCPAdoptionIPKit).  It has comprehensive guidance on what each step entails.

- Review the technical prerequisites and confirm readiness for GitHub [Organization](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/set-up/set-up-for-organization) or [Enterprise](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/set-up/set-up-for-enterprise)
- Assess business prerequisites (budget, permissions, approvals)
- Identify non-functional requirements (network, IDE extension rollout)
- Address questions on security, privacy, and compliance using [public customer terms](https://github.com/customer-terms)
- If the customer is not ready, recommend a [**GitHub Copilot workshop**](./intro.md) to build foundational knowledge
- Use the scoping call to identify educational topics and blockers to adoption
- Confirm skill levels, deployment targets, governance and rollout plans
- Agree on expectations, outcomes, session participants, and logistics

### Step 3: Delivery
Following the GitHub's `The Copilot Adoption Blueprint` guided framework.  The stages are onboarding, adoption, optimization, and sustain.  This delivery guide focuses on the onboarding and adoption phases.

#### Onboarding
Setting up the foundation for success. Click on each tab in order to learn more.

#### [1. Customer Kickoff](#tab/Onboarding-Customer-Kickoff)
A strong kick-off sets the tone for your rollout by aligning stakeholders, clarifying the vision, and building excitement for what’s ahead. It creates a shared understanding of goals, roles, and timelines to ensure everyone moves forward together.

#### [2. Define Measurable Goals](#tab/Onboarding-Define-Measurable-Goals)
Defining measurable goals ensures success can be tracked, celebrated, and continuously improved. Clear metrics keep teams focused on the outcomes that matter most and provide tangible proof of progress.

#### [3. Define Rollout Plan](#tab/Onboarding-Define-Rollout-Plan)
A well-structured rollout plan defines who will be onboarded when, outlines enablement milestones, and maps the path from pilot to full adoption. It keeps momentum high while minimizing risk through phased, intentional execution.

#### [4. Conduct Initial Set up](#tab/Onboarding-Conduct-Initial-Set-up)
Completing the initial setup ensures your technical foundations are in place—configurations, permissions, and integrations—so users can start with a smooth, reliable experience. It eliminates early blockers and sets the stage for successful adoption.

#### [5. Define Enablement Plan](#tab/Onboarding-Define-Enablement-Plan)
An enablement plan equips users with the training, resources, and support they need to confidently adopt and apply GitHub Copilot in their workflows. It ensures consistent learning experiences and accelerates time-to-value across teams. What you discovered in the scoping phase should help you define the right enablement plan.

#### [6. Identify Champions](#tab/Onboarding-Identify-Champions)
Starting your Champions Program empowers passionate early adopters to become advocates, educators, and role models within your organization. By equipping them with resources and recognition, you create momentum for sustained adoption and cultural change.


***
#### Adoption
Ramping up usage so we can realize value. Click on each tab in order to learn more.

#### [1. Launch Day](#tab/Adoption-Launch-Day)
Launch Day is the kickoff of your GitHub Copilot rollout. It's meant to generate excitement, establish the “why,” and ensure users are ready to dive in. It should include a mix of communication (email/slack), enablement (live demos), and access support (how to get started).

#### [2. Execute & Enhance Enablement Plan](#tab/Adoption-Execute-Enhance-Enablement-Plan)
Which ever enablement activities you defined in the onboarding phase, now is the time to execute them.  This may include live demos, office hours, workshops, and sharing resources.  You may also want to collect feedback from users to improve future sessions.

#### [3. Drive Engagement with Events](#tab/Adoption-Drive-Engagement-with-Events)
Driving engagement is about more than turning on Copilot—it’s about turning curiosity into consistent usage. This stage is your opportunity to spark energy, showcase impact, and build community around how developers are using GitHub Copilot in their real workflows.

#### [4. Build the internal Copilot Community](#tab/Adoption-Build-the-internal-Copilot-Community)
A strong internal community around GitHub Copilot accelerates learning, helps solve common challenges, and uncovers new ways to leverage AI in development. Peer-to-peer support and knowledge sharing are invaluable for widespread, effective adoption.  Communication templates via multiple channels.

#### [5. Monitor Success Metrics](#tab/Adoption-Monitor-Success-Metrics)
Monitoring success metrics ensures your Copilot rollout stays aligned to your defined measurable goals. With your KPIs already defined, this step focuses on tracking trends, surfacing insights, and sharing visibility across teams. It helps you spot what’s working, where to improve, and how to maintain momentum toward early ROI in the advanced stages.

***


### Step 4: Customer close out
Thank the customer for their time and identify next follow-up actions.

Recap the engagement and discuss next steps with customer stakeholders, including:

- Overview of GitHub Copilot and its value for the organization
- Review of engagement outcomes, learnings, and value delivered
  - Rollout and governance approach
  - User and administrator experience
  - Tasks completed and support provided
- Share productivity statistics and value realization evidence ([Report](https://github.blog/2022-09-07-research-quantifying-github-copilots-impact-on-developer-productivity-and-happiness/))
- Conduct demos for stakeholders as needed
