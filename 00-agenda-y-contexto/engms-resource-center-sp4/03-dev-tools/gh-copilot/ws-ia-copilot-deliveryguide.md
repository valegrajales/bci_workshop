# Delivery Guide: Workshop - GitHub - Copilot for Business Administration and Security
This guide will provides details and instructions to help you deliver the **Workshop - GitHub - Copilot for Business Administration and Security** for your customer. 
This engagement must accomplish the following objectives:  

**Objectives**
After this engagement, Enterprise/ Organisation admins will be able to:

- Enable the best & recommended policies & settings at an Enterprise/Organisation level
- Fetch all possible telemetry data from the GitHub instance
- Resolve the identified standard enablement challenges
- Communicate effectively on what all data is captured & how they are processed by GitHub
- Retrieve the GitHub Copilot for Business specific events
- Take care of the License management leveraging the REST APIs endpoints provided by GitHub

**Deliverable Standards**
Education session containing instructor sessions with slides, demos, and hands on labs.

## Customer Participants

Having the right customer participants in the  workshop is essential to completing a successful engagement. 
Ensure the customer roles outlined below are available and able to participate in the workshop. 

-  Developers
-  Team Leads
-  Engineering Managers
-  Product Security teams
-  DevSecOps teams
-  Org & Enterprise Administrators

## Delivery Guidance
This section includes all non-technical delivery guidance and supporting artifacts for this delivery. 
You are free to adapt each step to meet your customers needs as long as you are able to meet the objectives and deliverable standards for next MCEM phase. 

### Step 1: Preparing for the delivery

- ***For the best delivery experience, create you own GitHub Enterprise instance by following instructions toward the bottom of this guide.  Search for "Setting up your own GitHub Enterprise" in this document***

- You have reviewed the training outline and objectives as described in the slide deck available on the IP Share
  - the slide deck contains speaker notes for each topic
  - The IPShare also contains additional resources such as enablement decks and videos

- You have informed the customer about the training pre-requisites. 
  - For this delivery to be successful, GitHub Copilot for Business must be enabled on the supported target platforms, and Enterprise Owner/Organization Admin access needs to be available during the engagement. 
  - All participants need to have a corporate login to GitHub and a Copilot for Business license assigned.



- Gain access to the [GitHub demo environment](https://microsoft.sharepoint.com/:w:/r/teams/GithubSales/_layouts/15/Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=%7B264B191D-9345-4393-8A4C-63D73DFD0B49%7D&file=Onboarding%20Microsoft%20Users%20to%20the%20EMU.docx&action=default&mobileredirect=true)
- Gain access to [GitHub Copilot](https://aka.ms/copilot) for your work and linked personal account


- [Review the GitHub Copilot "Getting Started" resources](https://github.com/features/copilot/getting-started)
- [Review GitHub Copilot usage guidance by the Cloud Advocacy team](https://repos.opensource.microsoft.com/orgs/MicrosoftCopilot?section=videos)


### Step 2: Scoping

Explain how the delivery will be carried out, and what the customer can expect at each phase, before and after delivery. 
Make sure the right stakeholders from the customer's organization are present throughout the delivery. Refer to [Customer Participants](#customer-participants) section for details.

Ensure you discuss and understand the customer's requirements and that the tooling to be used is agreed. 
Ensure all the stakeholders are on the same page regarding the mode of delivery, the deliverables and the outcomes of the delivery.
Ensure that customer has GitHub Copilot enabled in their environment to practice any hands-on exercises.
Explain that without a Copilot license, customers can do limited hands-on practise with Free version.

### Step 3: Kickoff

Share details of what workshop activities you will cover and what will not be covered. 
Any agenda changes should already have been discussed during scoping, but the kickoff meeting is an opportunity to make any last minute changes and maximize customer satisfaction.
At this stage you are not required to make accomodations that aren't feasible. You should however consider anything that gets raised.

### Step 4: Delivery

- Make sure the customer understands that all participants need a GitHub account on the supported target platform with their corporate login and GitHub Copilot for Buiness enabled.
- **Introduction** of participants and providers
- Give a reminder that the enablement will be performed on the recent release version of Copilot and Copilot Chat
- Present and discuss the **agenda**
- Do the ice breaker for each training day to get to know the audience, encourage interactions, and learn expectations
- For each section in the slide deck, ensure that the following items are covered:
   - Give high level overview of the Copilot Fundamentals Recap from L100. This would include answering questions such as 'what is copilot', the requirements and data flow.
   - GitHub Copilot Enterprise tools available such as Copilot Chat, Copilot Docs, Copilot CLI, Copilot API, Copilot PR, and Copilot VS Code Extension.
   - Explain the Enterprise and Organizational settings and how they should be used.
   - Explain the telemetry audit logs, REST API, and how they should be used.
   - Go over GitHub Copilot's build in safeguards and how they should be used.
   - Understand common issue and how to troubleshoot them using GitHub Copilot.
   - Explain the integrations available with GitHub Copilot.
   - Demo the hands-on yourself to make sure nobody was lost and could not follow along.
   - Questions raised by the audience, either via chat or verbally, have been either addressed directly or noted for follow-up
   - Share the demo content with the audience to practice on their own and answer any questions they may have
- Do a retro at the end of each day to collect feedback and manage the expectations
- Once the session is done, do a final Q&A to collect any questions not addressed during the training

The following agenda items should be covered during the workshop:

| SECTION | SUMMARY | DURATION |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Introduction | Give a refresher of the fundamentals training and features of GitHub Copilot. | 20 minutes |
| Architecture & Data Flow | Review of GitHub Copilot's data flow. | 10 minutes |
| AI Controls & Policies | Review enterprise and organization settings, available IDE options & governance tips. | 30 minutes |
| Admin Customization & Break | Set up custom instructions, enablement tools, and learn what options you have | 50 minutes |
| Telemetry | Focus on how admins can gain valuable information about the usage of GitHub Copilot. | 25 minutes |
| Integrations for Admins | Managing integrations from Spaces to MCP. | 15 minutes |
| Troubleshooting | Guide through common issues and ways to fix them. | 20 minutes |
| Data Privacy | Taking a look at helpful data issues and the GitHub Trust Center | 15 minutes |
| Questions | Questions from the audience and answers from the presenter. | 20 minutes |

### Step 5: Customer close out

If needed send follow up materials / resources to the attendees. 
Ensure that the customer understands what the next steps are after the workshop activities. 
You need to understand the basics of VBD (such as survey procedures) and tell them what they can expect next. (in terms of communication from the CSAM or the account team)
