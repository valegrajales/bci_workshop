# Learn the Delivery

The **Upskilling Plan and Execution** engagement is designed to help identify knowledge gaps and build a digital capability and skilling plan that describes 
the knowledge customers need to achieve success with the adoption, operational, governance, and migration plans for this customer scenario.

On average the Upskilling Plan and Execution engagement takes 1 day of effort. This can be completed 
as a contiguous block of time or over a longer period of time depending on customer 
availability.

To deliver this engagement:
