# Initiate customer training

## Activity Description​

Provide any training that is needed as part of Technical Proof. Note that solution-based training for end users would be part of the Realize Value stage.   
 
## Roles 

Specialist, Account Executive/Account Technology Strategist, Training Program Manager.
 
## Outcomes

Training to support proof work has been organized and coordinated to coincide as part of proof work.
 
## Tasks

Coordinate with customer on how training can be delivered to the audience required as part of proof work.
 
## Content & Programs

No min content requirement at this stage.
