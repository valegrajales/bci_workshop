# Solution Accelerator – Azure AI Governance

## Activity Description

The **Solution Accelerator for Azure AI Governance** is a guided, 3-day engagement to align on enterprise AI governance for models, agents, and data using Azure AI reference patterns.

It combines:

- **Education (1.5 days):** Foundations, roles/RACI, governance pillars, and an operational readiness overview.
- **Implementation guidance (1.5 days):** Tailoring and delivery planning, plus an operational readiness working session that produces reusable outputs.

> Note: Content and depth should be scoped based on available time and priorities.

## Roles

Specialist, Technology Specialist, Cloud Solution Architect.

## Outcome

Reusable governance and operational readiness outputs that support decision-making and implementation planning, including:

- Draft operating model / RACI for agents, models, and data.
- Starter governance checklists/templates to adapt.
- Decision log and prioritized next steps across security/compliance, data sovereignty, cost controls, and monitoring.
- Possible implementations that are currently available:
	- Azure AI cost-governance guidance (setup, token and quota controls, and team-based chargeback/showback reporting).

## Task

Using the datasheet and delivery guide as the primary facilitation assets, engage customer stakeholders to:

- Facilitate modules and working sessions across the governance pillars and operational readiness.
- Capture decisions, owners, and sequencing; validate escalation paths and human-in-the-loop decision points.
- Produce the agreed outputs and align on immediate next steps.
