# Learning Path: Well-Architected Assessment for Azure AI

## Introduction

Welcome to the learning path for the Well-Architected Assessment for Azure AI. This learning path is designed to help you understand the key concepts, methodologies, and best practices for evaluating and improving your organization's AI readiness and strategy using Azure AI solutions.
By completing this learning path, you will gain a comprehensive understanding of the Well-Architected Assessment for Azure AI and be equipped with the knowledge and skills to evaluate and improve your organization's AI readiness and strategy.
