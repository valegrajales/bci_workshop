# WorkshopPLUS- Azure AI: Platform and Services

This workshop intends to provide foundational knowledge to the audience on the Azure AI Platform and Services and provides an opportunity to learn and benefit from a first experience with the product features, with guided hands-on labs. 



## Objectives and Outcomes

:point_down:
<details><summary><b>Click to expand to show more details.</b></summary>
</br>

**Objectives**
This workshop offers an opportunity for the audience to gain hands-on experience with the product features through guided labs.
* Introduction to concepts with presentation and demos specifically on Azure AI Services
* Labs for relevant modules with access to azure subscription for the duration of the workshop
* Helping the audience understand the value of Azure AI Services/Platform for their organization.
* Preparing the audience for their next steps, such as executing Proof of Concepts (POCs), architecting and designing solutions, and deploying solutions in production

**Outcomes**

After attending the sessions, customers will walk away with
- Knowledge to execute Proof of Concepts (POCs) for building solutions with Azure AI Services.
- Understanding of how to architect and design solutions using Azure AI Services.
- Skills to deploy these solutions in production environments

 - **AI Fundamentals:** This module introduces the Azure AI Platform and its suite of services, focusing on how these tools enable the development of intelligent, market-ready applications. Key topics include an overview of Azure AI Services such as Speech, Language, Vision, Search, and Document Intelligence, as well as the Azure OpenAI Service and Content Safety. The module explores practical use cases like speech-to-text, translation, sentiment analysis, document summarization, and content moderation. It also highlights responsible AI practices, security, compliance, and the integration of AI capabilities into business processes to drive innovation and ensure trustworthy, safe AI deployments

- **Fine Tuning:** This module introduces the fundamentals of fine-tuning large language models (LLMs), explaining what fine-tuning is, why it is used, and when it is most effective. It explores the differences between prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and fine-tuning, highlighting how these techniques can be combined for optimal results. The module details both general-purpose and industry-specific use cases, emphasizing the benefits of customization for scalability, reliability, and efficiency. It covers various fine-tuning methods available in Azure AI, including supervised, preference, and reinforcement fine-tuning, along with practical steps for deploying and evaluating fine-tuned models. It provides guidance on no-code fine-tuning using Azure AI Foundry, making the process accessible for a wide range of users.

- **Prompt Engineering:** This module provides a comprehensive introduction to prompt engineering for generative AI, focusing on both text and image models. It covers the fundamentals of what prompts are, their key components (instruction and context), and best practices for crafting effective prompts. It guides the participants through advanced techniques such as prompt chaining, few-shot learning, chain-of-thought prompting, and prompt chunking, all aimed at improving model outputs and reducing errors or hallucinations. It also explains when to use prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), or fine-tuning, depending on the use case. Special attention is given to implementing guardrails, specifying output structure, and adjusting model parameters to ensure safe, reliable, and high-quality AI responses. It includes practical guidance on image prompt engineering, emphasizing contextual specificity, task orientation, and clear output formatting to maximize the effectiveness of AI-driven solutions

- **AI Agentic Service:** This module explores the evolution and practical deployment of enterprise-grade AI agents using Azure AI Agent Service. It highlights how agentic AI shifts automation from simple task execution to autonomous, goal-driven systems capable of reasoning, acting, and learning. The presentation covers real-world use cases—such as customer support, sales, IT ticketing, license renewal, and security automation—demonstrating how multi-agent orchestration and memory management can streamline complex business processes. It also addresses technical and organizational challenges, emphasizing the importance of secure, scalable architectures and integration with enterprise data. Microsoft’s platform is showcased as a flexible solution, supporting both low-code and pro-code development, enabling organizations to rapidly build, customize, and deploy AI agents with enterprise-grade security and interoperability

- **Model Management:** This module provides a comprehensive guide to managing Azure OpenAI models, focusing on model retirement, migration strategies, and best practices for upgrades. It covers the differences between model retirement and deprecation, outlines migration execution steps, and compares various model options based on quality, cost, and performance. The module also details evaluation methods, benchmarking tools, and performance testing approaches to ensure robust deployment and monitoring of large language models in enterprise environments

- **AI Foundry:** This module introduces Azure AI Foundry as a unified platform that empowers developers and IT professionals to design, customize, and manage AI applications using a comprehensive set of tools, models, and services. It highlights how the platform accelerates AI innovation by enabling secure data integration, flexible model selection, and streamlined deployment within familiar development environment. This module also explains the benefits of using Provisioned Throughput Units (PTUs) in Azure AI, highlighting how they deliver predictable performance, scalability, and cost efficiency for enterprise AI workloads. It also covers deployment options, quota management, and best practices to help organizations optimize their AI infrastructure. This module also introduces the Azure AI Foundry Model Catalog, highlighting its role as a comprehensive hub for discovering, benchmarking, and deploying over 11,000 foundation, industry, and community AI models. It covers model selection, lifecycle management, deployment options, security, and integration with leading providers like Microsoft, OpenAI, Hugging Face, Meta, and NVIDIA, empowering users to innovate with enterprise-grade reliability and flexibility. This module also introduces Azure AI Content Understanding, a multi-modal AI service that processes documents, images, audio, and video to extract structured insights and enable automation, analytics, and advanced search scenarios. It covers core concepts, the content understanding framework, pro mode features, and practical use cases for building intelligent content analyzer

- **AI Language:** This module introduces Azure AI Language Services, focusing on natural language processing capabilities such as text extraction, classification, translation, and summarization. It explores core features like named entity recognition, sentiment analysis, PII detection, and language detection, highlighting their practical applications in real-world business scenarios. The module also emphasizes the importance of responsible AI practices, including privacy, security, and regulatory compliance when deploying language solutions. Finally, it offers guidance on integrating these services, selecting the right tools, and applying them through hands-on labs and real-world examples.

- **AI Speech:** This module provides an overview of AI speech technologies, focusing on both speech recognition and synthesis. The module covers the architecture and workflow of modern speech systems, including real-world use cases and best practices. The module explores integration methods for embedding speech capabilities into enterprise applications, emphasizing security and scalability. 

- **AI Vision:** The module on Azure AI Vision provides an overview of various Azure AI services related to computer vision, including Optical Character Recognition (OCR), Image Analysis, Custom Vision, Face Service, and Video Indexer. It covers the features, capabilities, and use cases of these services, highlighting their applications in enhancing digital presence, improving customer engagement, and ensuring responsible use of AI 

- **Responsible AI:** This module provides a comprehensive overview of Responsible AI (RAI) principles and their practical application within the Azure AI platform. It explores the importance of ethical AI development, highlighting key risks such as bias, security vulnerabilities, and content safety. The training covers frameworks like the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and introduces tools such as PyRIT for red teaming and evaluation metrics for assessing AI quality and fairness. Participants learn about governance, risk mitigation strategies, and best practices for content moderation and PII detection. The module emphasizes the need for ongoing evaluation, transparency, and human oversight to ensure trustworthy and compliant AI systems.

- **Vector Databases:** This module covers how vector databases enable semantic, context-aware search by storing and indexing high-dimensional embeddings alongside traditional data. They overcome the limitations of keyword and full-text search by allowing similarity search based on meaning, not just exact words. Azure supports vector search in Cosmos DB, PostgreSQL, and SQL, integrating with AI services for embedding generation. Indexing strategies like IVFFlat, HNSW, and DiskANN are used to optimize performance, scalability, and cost for different workloads. Vector search powers GenAI and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications, enabling more accurate, scalable, and intelligent AI solutions.
