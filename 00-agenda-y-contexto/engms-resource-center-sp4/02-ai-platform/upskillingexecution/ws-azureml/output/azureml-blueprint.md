# WorkshopPLUS - Azure Machine Learning Outcome Blueprint

## Outcome

Apply foundational and advanced machine learning techniques—including regression, classification, clustering, and time series analysis—to real-world business scenarios.

## Entry criteria

- Learn about the previous stage's status from the Account team, including the Architecture Design document.
- During the pre-scoping call, sync with CSAM and TS (Technical Specialist) to verify if a POC was already delivered.
- Having the right customer participants in the Architecture Design and Review Session is essential to completing a successful engagement. Ensure the customer roles outlined next are available and able to participate in the session.
- Setting the expectations (Goals, Timing, Prerequisites Check, Outcomes, Challenges) with the customer.
- Understand whether the customer has worked in Azure Machine Learning Service before and whether they have models deployed in production already
- Understand their goals of attending this workshop, what business problems are they solving with the knowledge gained from the workshop
- Understand how familiar the customer is with machine learning concepts like regression, classification, clustering, or time series forecasting
- Understand whether they have experience with model evaluation techniques such as cross-validation or regularization
- Understand whether they are more interested in supervised, unsupervised, or time series models?
- Understand whether they are familiar with Azure Machine Learning service and its components, including Azure Machine learning pipelines
- Understand whether they are interested in Advanced concepts such as Responsible AI, MLOPs, monitoring, integration of Azure ML with other services, etc. Leverage the table under the Delivery guidance that has the hours information to make decision on what modules to deliver at the time of scoping.
