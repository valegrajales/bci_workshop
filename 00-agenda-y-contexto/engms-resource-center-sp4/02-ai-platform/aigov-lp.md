# Learning Path - Solution Accelerator for Azure AI Governance

The **Solution Accelerator for Azure AI Governance** learning path provides a structured approach for building the knowledge required to plan and facilitate an enterprise AI governance solution accelerator.

Follow the steps below to build your knowledge and practical skills:

## 1. Introduction

**Goal:** Understand the purpose and scope of the Solution Accelerator for Azure AI Governance.

*   Review the datasheet: [Solution accelerator datasheet](aigov-datasheet.md)
*   Review the Delivery Guide: [Delivery Guide](aigov-deliveryguide.md)
*   Optional reference materials: [AI Landing Zones](https://aka.ms/ailz)

**Activity:** Summarize (in 2–3 sentences) the solution accelerator objective, audience, and key outputs.

## 2. Key Concepts and Architecture

**Goal:** Learn foundational principles and reference patterns that inform governance decisions.

*    [Cloud Adoption Framework – AI scenarios](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/scenarios/ai/)
*    [Azure Well-Architected – AI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/)
*    [Azure Architecture Center – AI & ML](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/ai-ml/)
*    Optional reference: [AI Landing Zones – Reference architectures](https://aka.ms/ailz#reference-architectures)
*    Optional reference: [AI Landing Zones – Design checklist](https://aka.ms/ailz#design-framework)

**Activity:** Draft a simple AI governance operating model diagram (people + process + platform), labeling the main components.

## 3. Professional Soft-Skills/Capabilities

**Goal:** Recognize essential soft skills for successful delivery and customer engagement.

*    Review these resources:
     *    [Data & AI Architectural Design](https://aka.ms/DataAI/ArchitecturalDesign)
     *    [Data & AI Business Value](https://aka.ms/DataAI/BusinessValue)
     *    [Data & AI Situation Fluency](https://aka.ms/DataAI/SituationFluency)
     *    [Data & AI Trusted Advisership](https://aka.ms/DataAI/TrustedAdvisership)
     *    [Data & AI Digital Signals](https://aka.ms/DataAI/DigitalSignals)
       
**Activity:** Select one soft skill and describe a scenario where it would be important in an AI governance solution accelerator.

## 4. Preparation (Recommended)

**Goal:** Learn what inputs and stakeholders are needed to make the engagement productive.

*   Review the preparation checklist in the Delivery Guide: [Delivery Guide](aigov-deliveryguide.md)
*   Identify what customer inputs you need to request:
     *   Governance inputs (policies, standards, audit requirements, existing RACI)
     *   AI inventory (key models, agents, datasets, environments)
     *   Constraints (regulated data, regions, approval processes)

**Activity:** Draft a 1-page pre-engagement request (stakeholders + documents + inventory questions).

## 5. Solution Accelerator Agenda (3 Days)

**Goal:** Be able to facilitate the agenda flow from the datasheet and delivery guide (education + implementation guidance).

*   Review the agenda structure:
      *   Education (1.5 days)
           *   Day 1: Module 1, Module 2, Module 3 (Governance Pillars overview)
           *   Day 2 (AM): Module 3 (continue) + Module 4 (Education overview)
      *   Implementation Guidance (1.5 days)
           *   Day 2 (PM): Tailoring and delivery planning
           *   Day 3: Module 4 (Working session) + next steps

**Activity:** Write 3 discussion prompts for each agenda segment that drive decisions (not implementation details).

## 6. Solution Accelerator Outputs (Deliverables)

**Goal:** Learn how to produce and validate the expected solution accelerator outputs.

*   Align on the deliverables defined in the Delivery Guide:
     *   Draft RACI/operating model for agents, models, and data
     *   Starter governance checklist/template set to adapt
     *   Decision log and prioritized next steps (owners + sequencing)
     *   Possible implementations that are currently available:
          *   Azure AI cost-governance guidance (setup, token and quota controls, and team-based chargeback/showback reporting)

**Activity:** Create a starter deliverables pack (blank RACI, checklist skeleton, decision log template).

## 7. Close Out and Quality Checks

**Goal:** Develop skills in validating engagement outputs and resolving common alignment gaps.

*   Validate: Confirm RACI clarity, decision logs, and ownership of next steps
*   Troubleshoot: Identify common gaps (missing stakeholders, unclear policies, unknown data boundaries)
*   Close out: Confirm owners, timelines, approvals, and follow-up meetings

**Activity:** Document one validation step you performed to ensure outputs are actionable.
