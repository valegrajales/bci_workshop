# Learning Path - Landing Zone Accelerator to Build and Deploy Azure AI Platform​​​

The **AI Landing Zone Accelerator** engagement provides a structured approach for designing, deploying, and validating an enterprise-ready Azure AI platform. This enables you to demonstrate the feasibility of the architecture, including complete scenarios and integration with existing infrastructure, solutions, and applications.

Follow the steps below to build your knowledge and practical skills:

## 1. Introduction

**Goal:** Understand the purpose and scope of the Azure AI Landing Zone Accelerator.

*   Explore the AI Landing Zones solution materials: [AI Landing Zones – Solution Accelerator Repo](https://aka.ms/ailz)
*   Review the Delivery Guide: [Delivery Guide](lza-aiplat-deliveryguide.md)

**Activity:** Summarize in a few sentences the main objective of the Landing Zone Accelerator.

## 2. Key Concepts and Architecture

**Goal:** Learn the foundational architecture and design principles.

*    [Cloud Adoption Framework – AI scenarios](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/scenarios/ai/)
*    [Azure Well-Architected – AI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/well-architected/ai/)
*    [AI Landing Zones – Reference architectures](https://aka.ms/ailz#reference-architectures)
*    [AI Landing Zones – Design checklist](https://aka.ms/ailz#design-framework)

**Activity:** Draw or annotate a diagram of the AI Landing Zone architecture, labeling the main components.

## 3. Professional Soft-Skills/Capabilities

**Goal:** Recognize essential soft skills for successful delivery and customer engagement.

*    Review these resources:
     *    [Data & AI Architectural Design](https://aka.ms/DataAI/ArchitecturalDesign)
     *    [Data & AI Business Value](https://aka.ms/DataAI/BusinessValue)
     *    [Data & AI Situation Fluency](https://aka.ms/DataAI/SituationFluency)
     *    [Data & AI Trusted Advisership](https://aka.ms/DataAI/TrustedAdvisership)
     *    [Data & AI Digital Signals](https://aka.ms/DataAI/DigitalSignals)
       
**Activity:** Select one soft skill and describe a scenario where it would be important in an AI Landing Zone engagement.

## 4. Explore the Solution Accelerator Repository

**Goal:** Get familiar with the official codebase and documentation.

*   Access the main repository: <https://github.com/Azure/AI-Landing-Zones>
*   Review folders, scenarios, modules, and templates
*   Read deployment guides and README files

**Activity:** Locate the deployment guide for Bicep or Terraform in the repository.

## 5. Hands-On Deployment

**Goal:** Practice deploying the AI Landing Zone using Infrastructure as Code.

*   Deploy using Bicep: <https://aka.ms/ailz/bicep>
*   Deploy using Terraform: <https://aka.ms/ailz/terraform>
*   Customize parameters as needed for your environment

**Activity:** Deploy a test environment using Bicep or Terraform and verify the resources in the Azure Portal.

## 6. Deep Dive: Customization and Best Practices

**Goal:** Learn how to adapt the accelerator to different scenarios and requirements.

*   Study the design checklist and reference architectures
*   Review security, networking, and governance best practices from the documentation
*   Explore extensibility options (e.g., how to modify templates for your organization’s needs)

**Activity:** Modify your deployment to reflect a specific scenario (e.g., different networking setup, security policies). Explain your choices.

## 7. Validation and Troubleshooting

**Goal:** Develop skills in validating deployments and resolving common issues.

*   Validate: Check deployed resources in the Azure Portal
*   Troubleshoot: Use Azure diagnostics and monitoring tools
*   Learn: Review common errors (e.g., resource provider registration, network configuration) and how to fix them

**Activity:** Document a troubleshooting or validation step you performed after deployment.
